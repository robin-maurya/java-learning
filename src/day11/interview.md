# Day 11 - Polymorphism Interview Questions

## 1. What is Polymorphism?

Polymorphism means one thing having many forms.

---

## 2. What are the types of Polymorphism in Java?

- Compile-time Polymorphism
- Runtime Polymorphism

---

## 3. How is Compile-time Polymorphism achieved?

Through Method Overloading.

---

## 4. How is Runtime Polymorphism achieved?

Through Method Overriding.

---

## 5. What is Method Overloading?

Having multiple methods with the same name but different parameters.

---

## 6. What is Method Overriding?

A child class provides its own implementation of a method inherited from the parent class.

---

## 7. What is Dynamic Method Dispatch?

It is the process where Java determines the overridden method to execute at runtime based on the actual object.

---

## 8. What is the difference between Overloading and Overriding?

Overloading occurs at compile time, while overriding occurs at runtime.
